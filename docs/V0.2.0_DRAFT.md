# V0.2.0 Draft

Date: 2026-04-05

## Scope

`v0.2.0` formalizes the attack layer as a first-class subsystem and upgrades the stress-analysis pipeline from ad hoc attack hooks to reproducible, parameterized attack injection plus matched clean-vs-stress comparison.

The release target is the natural-quarantine question:

Does a topology that degrades useful fact transmission (`F`) also suppress malicious propagation (`infection_spread`)?

### 2. Strict Attack Metrics

The calibrated `F`, `rho`, `B`, `C` extractors were not modified.

Instead, `v0.2.0` adds a separate stress-analysis layer:

- `infection_spread_rate`
- `attack_success_rate`
- `quarantine_strength`
- `F_delta_vs_clean`
- `score_delta_vs_clean`

Definition used in `summary.json`:

`quarantine_strength = 1.0 - infection_spread_rate`

This treats ideal direct transmission as normalized full spread across all non-attacker nodes.

Matched clean-vs-stress enrichment is now implemented in:

- [src/coord_harness/analysis/attack_analysis.py](d:/Dev/TEST_HYPO/src/coord_harness/analysis/attack_analysis.py)

and persisted to:

- `summary.attack_analysis`
- `attack_analysis_report.json`

MA-FT aggregate, budgets `32` and `96`, all models and both seeds:

- `balanced_tree`: mean `F_delta_vs_clean = 0.161458`, mean `infection_spread_rate = 0.126953`, mean `quarantine_strength = 0.873047`, mean `score_delta_vs_clean = 0.03125`
- `star`: mean `F_delta_vs_clean = 0.5`, mean `infection_spread_rate = 0.148438`, mean `quarantine_strength = 0.851562`, mean `score_delta_vs_clean = -0.0625`

Budget slices for the same MA-FT subset:

- `budget 32`: `balanced_tree` has `F_delta = 0.177083`, `infection = 0.144531`; `star` has `F_delta = 0.46875`, `infection = 0.15625`
- `budget 96`: `balanced_tree` has `F_delta = 0.145834`, `infection = 0.109375`; `star` has `F_delta = 0.53125`, `infection = 0.140625`

Global matched-point correlation from the systematic sweep:

- overall `pearson_r = 0.197333` for `F_delta_vs_clean` vs `infection_spread_rate`, `n = 32`
- `budget 96`: `pearson_r = 0.334597`
- `budget 32`: `pearson_r = 0.066454`

Interpretation:

The systematic live run does not support the intended negative natural-quarantine law. If anything, the sign is weakly positive: larger `F` degradation tends to co-occur with higher infection spread, not lower infection spread.

What the data does support is narrower and more defensible:

- `balanced_tree` preserves more useful fact flow than `star`
- `balanced_tree` also slightly reduces infection spread relative to `star`
- the mechanism is therefore not "sacrifice useful coordination to gain quarantine"
- the stronger observed pattern is "star is worse on both fronts"
